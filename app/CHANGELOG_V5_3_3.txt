MFCase 5.3.3
- EXPORT COMPLETED exports all approved unsent PDFs, regardless of row selection. Files needing review remain. Exported rows leave the list only after successful transfer.
- Leading zeros are preserved exactly in export filenames. 08-01114 stays 08-01114. Collision suffixes remain safe.
- A persisted scan-completion barrier prevents exports before the final before/after pass, including after stopping/reopening a scan. Resume SCAN to complete it. Matching neighbors may correct a conflicting 100% read; multiple conflicts require manual review. Manual corrections remain protected.
- Added direct full-line EasyOCR recognition on raw, enlarged and padded number crops, avoiding a second text-detection step that can omit narrow digits. No invented 1s, character replacements, confidence inflation or zero stripping.
- Tightened the vertical crop for an additional case-field read to reduce interference from the OFFENSE CODES row.
- Existing two-engine approval thresholds remain: matching complete 08- case, both above 75%, one above 95%. Unresolved disagreements still require review.

Validation: 44 automated regression tests, including real temporary-file exports, untouched review files, leading zeros, collision handling, scan-completion gating and updater rollback. Local EasyOCR checks recovered the screenshot's printed 08-0119 and both printed 08-120 crops.
