MFCase 5.3.8
- Strip leading zeros only from final filename case digits. Keep a single zero
  for an all-zero suffix. OCR, confirmed values and training labels unchanged.
- Exact string comparison for anchors and OCR ambiguities. Differently padded
  readings no longer silently count as identical evidence.
- Rebuild before/after inference from frozen independent anchors on every check.
  Withdraw stale approvals when boundaries change, disappear or conflict.
- Automatically recheck after green approval, SAVE REVIEW, edits, unverify and
  restoring the queue. Inferred results never become new anchors.
- Mandatory fresh sequence check immediately before selecting rename jobs.
  Check or persistence failure blocks renaming.
- Persist original inference evidence for reliable revalidation after restart.
- Existing filenames are not silently rewritten when an inference is withdrawn;
  the next explicit rename handles corrected approved names, with collision safety.
- UNVERIFY no longer deletes historical learning examples or crops.
Validation: 91 regression tests. No recognition-model or training-loop changes.
Native Windows GUI and real-document OCR accuracy remain unverified.
