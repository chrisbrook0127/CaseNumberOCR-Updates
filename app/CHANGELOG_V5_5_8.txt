MFCase v5.5.8 — Four-by-four automatic OCR lockdown

- Automatic OCR reads only the upper-right 4-inch-square region on each PDF page.
- Both handwritten and printed 08- case numbers remain supported through EasyOCR and TrOCR.
- INCIDENT NO./INCIDENT NUMBER/INCIDENT # labels remain optional locating clues.
- The old reviewer-learned full-page fallback is disabled for ALL automatic scans.
- Invalid or ambiguous OCR within 4x4 requires manual review; do not use unrelated text elsewhere.
- Manual review still shows the entire page and permits boxes anywhere.
- Fixed 95% approval, scan speed improvements, before/after, learning records, and SEND FILES remain.
- Existing saved outside-region learning records are preserved, but cannot drive automatic scans.
